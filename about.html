SYNTEGRA LIMITED is an established and trusted Electrical and Mechanical contracting company offering different types of solutions for both residential,commercial and industrial clients within the East Africa Region.

We are registered as Electrical and Mechanical contractors with relevant governing bodies namely Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority license class A1 and National Construction Authority. We also pride ourselves in having in-house engineers registered with the Engineers Board of Kenya and electricians licensed by the Energy and Petroleum Regulatory Authority.
