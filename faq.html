What services does Syntegra Limited offer?

Syntegra Limited provides electrical, mechanical and plumbing services, including building fit-outs, maintenance, remedial repairs, lighting, electrical rewiring, structured cabling, audio-visual installations and EPC services.

02

Does Syntegra Limited serve residential and commercial clients??

Yes. We provide engineering and contracting solutions for residential, commercial and industrial clients across Kenya and the East African region.

03

Does Syntegra Limited provide EPC services?

Yes. We provide Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) services, managing projects from engineering and procurement through installation, testing, commissioning and handover.

04

Is Syntegra Limited licensed and professionally qualified?

Yes. Syntegra Limited is registered with relevant regulatory bodies, including EPRA and NCA. Our team also includes engineers registered with the Engineers Board of Kenya (EBK) and EPRA-licensed electricians.

05

Why choose Syntegra Limited?

We combine technical expertise, quality workmanship and industry knowledge to deliver reliable, practical and cost-effective engineering solutions tailored to our clients' requirements and budgets.
